About Moon and Moss ❧

Moon and Moss Apothecary is a small, handmade skincare and bath product business created with love for sensitive skin. Having sensitive skin myself, I want to create and share natural and affordable products that help you feel good in your skin. Every product is made in small batches with gentle, natural ingredients because self care should be simple, safe, and for everyone.
